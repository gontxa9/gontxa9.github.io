    The breakfast burritos have names

    Meet the regulars.

    Most come stuffed with hashbrowns, eggs and cheese. Red sauce is the hot one, green is not.

    Asada, bacon, sausage, ham, avocado, hashbrowns, eggs, cheese, with sour cream, red and green sauce.

    Dr. Sesma
