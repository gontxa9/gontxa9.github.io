Jasmine

Burmese & halal kitchen · Burmese market · Culver City

A family-run spot on Sepulveda where Myanmar meets South Asia. Mohinga and tea leaf salad on one side of the menu, curries, biryani and tandoori from the clay oven on the other. And a little Burmese grocery to browse while you wait.
