Build your platter

Ethiopian food comes on one big round of injera, a soft, spongy flatbread, with each dish spooned on top. Tap the dishes to see how your table would look.

On your platter

    How to eat it: tear off a piece of injera with your right hand, pinch up some of a dish and eat it in one bite. More injera comes on the side.
    The menu

    Every dish comes with injera

    A guide to what's cooking at Awash.

    Meat & fish

    Awaze TibbsTender beef cooked with spices and awaze, a red pepper sauce.
    Special TibbsChunks of beef sautéed with onions, garlic and jalapeños.
    Alitcha TibbsThe milder tibbs, with a touch of turmeric.
    Zilzil TibbsLong strips of beef seared on the charcoal grill with garlic, black pepper and spiced butter.
    KitfoEthiopian steak tartare: chopped beef with spiced butter and mitmita. Special kitfo adds garlic, onion and jalapeño.
    Tibbs FirfirInjera mixed into saucy awaze tibbs.
    Whole fried fishServed whole, with lime.

    Chicken

    Awaze Chicken TibbsChicken cooked with spices and awaze.
    Chicken TibsCubes of chicken sautéed with onions, garlic and spices, with mixed vegetables.
    Grilled ChickenThigh fillets seasoned with garlic and black pepper, grilled over charcoal.
    Chicken CutletThigh fillets sautéed with lemon and garlic.

    Fish & vegetarian comboThe whole fish with the vegetable dishes.
