Why "Nam Won"

Named after the town famous for this soup

Namwon is a town in Korea's Jeolla province, and its name is tied to chueotang. "Nam Won Gol" means, roughly, "Namwon village".

Here the mudfish is ground right into the broth: a deep, savory soup that regulars say has no fishy taste at all. Cheonggukjang, the Jeolla-style soybean stew, is on the menu too.
