    The secret is the edges

    Hold the button.
    Smash the patty.

    One reviewer put it best: the patty is “thin and slightly crisp around the edges.” Press and hold to smash it on the griddle. Let go too early and it's thick. Hold it right and you get the lacy, crunchy edge people keep coming back for.
